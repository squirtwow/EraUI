⚔️ EraUI: Classic WoW, restored.

💬 Support, bugs & ideas: join our Discord to get help, report issues, suggest features and share your UI.

The 2004 interface you remember, rebuilt for World of Warcraft: Forever, with the quality-of-life you'd miss. Every feature is optional.

✦ Highlights

  • 🖼️ The real classic look: action bar, unit frames, cast bars, minimap, nameplates and classic-styled windows
  • 🎛️ Presets: Classic + Quality of Life, Classic look only, or Quality of Life only to keep Blizzard's modern interface. Pick one in the setup, or switch any time with Presets in /era
  • 🗺️ Movable world map: drag it by the header and resize it from the corners or edges, scaling the whole window. It stays where you put it, even in combat; normal and expanded layouts are remembered separately, and the quest list overlays the right side without squeezing the map artwork
  • 🌍 Reveal World Map: show supported unexplored terrain with darker shading, while explored areas keep their normal colours
  • 🎯 Crowd control on portraits: icons and countdowns on player, target, focus and party portraits, in combat too
  • 📊 Classic damage meter: an optional Classic look for Blizzard's damage meter, including extra windows and the spell breakdown
  • 🔔 Class reminders: class-coloured, draggable alerts for missing buffs, pets, weapon coatings and supplies, with Not enough mana (or rage) until you can cast
  • 📖 Training Guide: an optional tab inside the spellbook, populated immediately with unlearned spells, required levels, availability groups and reference cost totals. Spells taught by class quests name the quest and where it starts
  • 🔤 Damage & healing text: pick the font of the numbers, your own heals included (Pepsi by default, plus 13 more), and their size
  • 🔗 Click links again to close: a second click on an item or spell link in chat closes its tooltip
  • 📣 Update notice: EraUI tells you in chat when someone in your guild or group has a newer version
  • 💤 AFK Screen: when you go AFK, the EraUI banner in your class colour, your character, your details and a famous Classic line for your class. On by default
  • 🎒 Bag Item Levels: item levels in quality colours, upgrade arrows and a red icon on gear you can't equip (optional)
  • 🔴 Combo points your way: floating orbs in gold or red
  • 🖱️ Cursor effects: independently optional cursor ring, cast-progress ring and fading trail, with adjustable appearance
  • 🧹 Full-bag junk cleanup: optional cheapest-grey-stack deletion while looting, with a linked chat message and Shift bypass
  • 🧰 Quality of life everywhere: vendor prices, bag space, auto-repair, swing timers, class tools and more
  • 📣 "What's new" after each update: a class-coloured, screen-fitting window with scrollable release notes, mouse-wheel support and an always-visible Got it button. Open recent notes anytime using Changelog at the bottom of /era

🎛️ Presets

  • Classic + Quality of Life: the 2004 look, plus your choice of Quality of Life options. Recommended
  • Classic look only: the 2004 look; your Quality of Life options stay as they are
  • Quality of Life only: keep Blizzard's modern interface and use EraUI's Quality of Life options
  • Choose on the first page of /era setup, or switch any time with the Presets button at the top of /era, which shows your current preset. Switching tells you how many settings change and reloads your UI
  • Presets only switch the Classic look, including Other Windows; your Quality of Life choices are never changed
  • Options that need a Classic piece, such as the Training Guide (Classic Spellbook) or Important Debuffs on Portraits (Classic Unit Frames), are greyed out while it is off

  • Tooltips: translucent dark backgrounds and thin, bevelled grey borders with softened corners on item, spell, unit and map-control hover boxes, including linked items and item comparisons. Enabled by default for fresh settings and resets; existing saved choices are respected. Toggle Tooltips in /era and reload to apply. Optional Class-coloured Tooltips show a player's border, name and class in their class colour

🖱️ Cursor effects

  • Find the controls under /era → Text & Camera
  • Cursor Ring: a ring follows your pointer, with adjustable diameter and thickness and an optional class colour
  • Cast Progress Ring: a thin ring fills during casts and drains during channels. When Cursor Ring is enabled, it stays outside that ring with a small gap. Interruptions simply hide it, without a red flash
  • Cursor Trail: a short, soft trail follows movement and fades away when the pointer stops
  • The cast ring and trail each support class or custom colours and adjustable size and opacity. Trail fade length is also adjustable
  • All three effects can be enabled or disabled independently and start off by default

🔤 Damage & healing text

  • Find it under /era → Text & Camera, or on its own page in /era setup
  • Pick the font of the damage and healing numbers over your targets, your own heals included: Pepsi by default, Bangers, Luckiest Guy, Lilita One, Titan One, Black Ops One, Russo One, Bungee, Permanent Marker, Press Start 2P, and the game's Skurri, Morpheus, Friz Quadrata and Arial Narrow
  • A new font shows after you log out and back in. If you pick one during /era setup, the last page offers to log you out
  • The size slider shows a sample number and applies straight away
  • Fonts: Pepsi by Jakub Degórski; the others from Google Fonts under their own open licences

🎯 Important Debuffs on Portraits

  • Find this optional feature under /era → HUD. Requires Unit Frames
  • Shows crowd control on player, target, focus and party unit portraits only, with the game's own icon, cooldown sweep and countdown, in and out of combat
  • Enemy targets show the most important effect: stuns, then fear/incapacitation, silences and roots. Friendly units show any crowd control, and your own portrait shows what is controlling you
  • While shown, the icon covers the portrait's level badge; your level returns when the effect ends
  • Ordinary movement slows are not shown. Off by default

🧹 Full-bag junk cleanup

  • Enable Discard Cheapest Junk When Bags Are Full under /era → Automation. It starts off by default
  • EraUI checks your bags automatically. You do not need to open them manually
  • When bags are full, right-clicking a corpse the client identifies as lootable can free the cheapest grey stack during that click. If loot rights are unavailable, cleanup waits for a bags-full loot error and your next normal world or loot click
  • Compares total stack vendor value, with at most one stack discarded per loot window
  • Runs outside combat, skips locked and quest items, and leaves an occupied cursor alone. Unknown prices prevent choosing a potentially more expensive stack
  • Hold Shift to bypass. No separate deletion confirmation is needed; a linked local chat message reports the discarded item and quantity
  • Automatic looting gets one retry after space is freed; manual looting stays manual

🔔 Class reminders & buffs

  • Open your class tab in /era, enable Class Reminders & Buffs, and use the options directly below it
  • Drag each alert independently, choose Small, Normal or Large, and reset positions when needed
  • Use Show all reminders to preview and arrange applicable alerts
  • Pick a preferred aspect, blessing, seal, aura, shaman weapon imbue or warlock demon under Advanced
  • Hunters have separate SUMMON PET! and PET DEAD! reminders, using Call Pet and Revive Pet icons; each can be toggled under Advanced. Selected warlock demons are checked by demon family
  • Optional Clickable reminders (outside combat) starts off. Enable it to left-click supported reminder icons to cast their spell, including Call Pet and Revive Pet; drag the text to reposition. Preview alerts never cast
  • Hunters and warlocks can also turn on Click pet reminders in combat to click SUMMON PET!, PET DEAD! and PET LOW HEALTH! mid-fight
  • Blessing, shaman imbue and warlock demon reminders left on Any cast the only one you know, or the one you cast last; or pick a specific one under Advanced. A buff you're missing yourself is cast on you, even with someone else targeted; otherwise targetable group buffs use your friendly living target. Reminders without an associated spell remain informational
  • When you don't have the mana (or a warrior's rage) for a reminder's spell, Not enough mana (or rage) shows under it and it can't be clicked until you can cast it. Turn this off with Say when you're out of mana (out of rage for warriors)
  • Weapon reminders distinguish the main and off hand, skip empty slots and shields, and check known poison or imbue types
  • All reminders hide while you're on a flight path and come back a moment after you land
  • Optional PET LOW HEALTH! for hunters and warlocks shows below 35% by default (20 to 60%), in combat too with Show during combat. With Clickable reminders, clicking it out of combat casts Mend Pet or Health Funnel
  • Shamans can enable a separate Windfury Totem reminder under Advanced; the general totem reminder checks whether any totem is active
  • Optional party/raid checks skip duplicate player entries and members known to be dead, offline or out of range; party-only buffs check your raid subgroup
  • Options follow class capabilities: ineffective Rogue/Shaman group checks and unsupported Rogue reminder clicking are hidden
  • Dedicated Rogue Poison Reminders handles missing-poison alerts while enabled, avoiding duplicate generic alerts while retaining low-time and low-charge warnings. Poison Supplies remains a separate purchase/crafting tool
  • Soulstone application checks whether a checked member is protected, rather than requiring it on everyone
  • Alerts are silent and hidden during combat by default. Show during combat enables informational alerts; click actions always stay disabled during combat

🐾 Hunter feeding

  • Enable Pet Feeding in /era under Hunter; a feeding button appears outside combat when Feed Pet is learned and your living pet's happiness is low
  • Configure food preferences under Pet Feeding Options in the Hunter tab
  • Right-click the icon to unlock, drag it to move, scroll to resize and left-click to lock
  • Once locked, left-click feeds your pet with the selected food
  • Move / resize feeding button opens a positioning preview, including before Feed Pet is learned
  • The floating icon stays hidden while browsing settings; the Hunter panel explains pet-related visibility conditions

🧙 Class-tool settings

  • Class-reminder controls are available directly on each class page, without redundant Configure shortcuts
  • Expanded class options and search results scroll within the settings window, keeping the footer accessible
  • A centred Scroll down for more options hint appears when additional settings are below the viewport
  • Mage Conjuring & Trade Options includes one-click conjuring outside combat and configurable food/water trade stack counts
  • Mage conjure buttons hide during combat; settings navigation, scrolling and closing remain available

📖 Training Guide

  • Training Guide is enabled by default for fresh settings and resets; existing saved choices are respected. Its tab appears below the class icons and displays inside the Classic spellbook, which must also be enabled. Toggle Training Guide in /era and reload to apply
  • Bundled spell metadata for all nine Forever classes populates the list immediately, without visiting a trainer
  • Unverified seasonal/default learning levels are excluded rather than displayed as level-1 training
  • Search unlearned spells and ranks, with groups for Available Now, Available but Missing Requirements, Coming Soon and Not Yet Available
  • See required levels and separate reference totals for current and all listed unlearned spells
  • Cost references use Classic base prices and may differ on Forever; unverified prices are marked ? and excluded from the priced subtotal
  • Optional trainer visits update recorded quotes, including discounts, per character and realm
  • Spells taught by class quests (Bear Form, Teleport: Moonglade, warrior stances, hunter pet skills, shaman totems, warlock demons and more) show Quest: with the quest's name and where it starts, for your race and faction, including Skyborne. Hover to see who gives it. They don't count as unknown prices
  • Learned spells disappear from the list; return to a class tab to use the normal spellbook

💬 Commands

  • /era opens the settings window. Use Presets at the top to switch presets, and the Changelog button at the bottom for recent release notes
  • /era setup replays the guided first-run setup, starting with the presets
  • /era welcome shows the welcome screen again
  • /era updates shows the latest update notes again
  • /era reminders opens class-reminder settings
  • /era discord gives the Discord invite to copy, for bugs and ideas. The bottom of /era and the What's new window have a Discord button too
  • /era status opens a selectable diagnostics report
  • /era-classic status opens the detailed status/error-report window, including the Discord button and a copyable support invite
  • /era audit runs the addon audit report
  • /era mapdebug opens map diagnostics
  • /era reset restores default settings and reloads automatically; use it outside combat

Most options apply immediately; the panel marks the few that need a reload. General settings and reminder layouts are shared across your characters. Swing toggles and class-tool preferences are remembered per character and realm. Settings and supplementary panels use your class colour. Quiet Mode is on by default.
